# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.11.05

Astral Colony (“the Game”) respects your privacy.

The Game does not require user accounts, registration, or the submission of personal information.

## Information We Collect

The Game does not directly collect personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Address
- Precise location
- Account information

Game-related data may be stored locally on your device, including:

- Game progress
- Stage progress
- Unlocked content
- Game settings
- Tutorial and gameplay state

This data is used only to provide and maintain the game experience.

## Third-Party Platforms

If the Game is distributed through a third-party platform such as Steam, that platform may process information according to its own privacy policy.

The developer does not independently collect or store personal information received from those platforms unless otherwise stated.

## Data Storage

The Game does not operate its own user account system or personal data server.

Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is deleted, reset, or removed when the Game is uninstalled.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game’s features or data handling practices change.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
