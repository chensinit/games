# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.08.21

Alicia: The Retired Slayer (“the App”) respects your privacy.

The App is a fantasy RPG featuring character progression, equipment, battles, story content, and animated Live Home scenes.

The App does not require user accounts, login, registration, or user-provided personal information.

However, the App displays advertisements, and advertising partners may collect or process certain information to provide, measure, and improve ads.


## Information We Collect

The App itself does not directly collect personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Address
- Precise location
- User account information

The App may store game-related data locally on your device, such as:

- Game progress
- Stage and story progress
- Character progression
- Equipment and upgrade data
- In-game settings
- Gameplay preferences
- Unlocked content

This locally stored data is used only to provide and maintain the game experience and is not used by the App to personally identify users.


## Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information from your device, such as:

- Advertising ID
- Device information
- App interaction information
- Approximate location derived from IP address
- Ad performance information
- Diagnostic information

This information may be used to:

- Display advertisements
- Limit repeated advertisements
- Measure advertising performance
- Detect fraud and abuse
- Improve advertising services
- Provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on user settings, region, and consent requirements

For more information about how Google collects and uses data, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising privacy information.


## Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party service:

- Google AdMob

Third-party services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

The App developer does not control the data collection or processing practices of third-party service providers.


## App Functionality

The App is a fantasy role-playing game featuring:

- Story-driven gameplay
- Character progression
- Equipment acquisition
- Equipment enhancement
- Battle content
- Animated Live Home scenes
- Unlockable game content

All characters, stories, battles, items, equipment, currencies, and other game content are fictional and provided for entertainment purposes only.

Game progress and related gameplay data may be stored locally on the user’s device.


## Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

However, advertisements may be displayed through third-party advertising services.

If the App is made available to children or users under the applicable age of consent, appropriate child-directed, age-appropriate, or family-safe advertising settings should be applied according to applicable laws, Google Play policies, and advertising platform requirements.


## Data Security

The App does not operate its own user account system and does not store user-provided personal information on its own servers.

Locally stored game data remains on the user’s device unless otherwise stated.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is handled according to their respective privacy and security practices.


## User Choices

Users may be able to control or reset advertising identifiers through their device settings.

Users may also manage advertising personalization and privacy settings through Google or their device settings, depending on the operating system and region.

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with consent options regarding personalized advertising.

Users may delete locally stored game data by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App, depending on device settings.


## Data Retention

The App does not independently retain personal information on its own servers.

Locally stored game data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, cleared through device settings, or removed when the App is uninstalled.

Data processed by third-party services may be retained according to their respective privacy policies.


## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the App’s features, third-party services, or data handling practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
