# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.06.24

철들었네 (“the App”) respects your privacy.

The App does not require user accounts, login, registration, or user-provided personal information. However, the App displays advertisements, and advertising partners may collect or process certain information to provide, measure, and improve ads.

## Information We Collect

The App itself does not directly collect personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Address
* Precise location
* User account information

## Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information from your device, such as:

* Advertising ID
* Device information
* App interaction information
* Approximate location derived from IP address
* Ad performance and diagnostic information

This information may be used to:

* Show ads
* Limit repeated ads
* Measure ad performance
* Detect fraud and abuse
* Improve advertising services
* Provide personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on user settings and consent requirements

For more information about how Google uses data, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising privacy information.

## Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party service:

* Google AdMob

Third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

## App Functionality

The App provides seasonal food information based on Korean seasonal food data.

All app content is provided for general informational purposes only. Seasonal food periods may vary depending on region, variety, farming method, fishing conditions, distribution timing, and other factors.

## Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

However, advertisements may be displayed through third-party advertising services. If the App is made available to children or users under the applicable age of consent, appropriate child-directed or family-safe ad settings should be applied according to store and advertising platform policies.

## Data Security

The App does not operate its own user account system or store user-provided personal information on its own servers.

Information processed by advertising partners is handled according to their respective privacy and security practices.

## User Choices

Users may be able to control or reset advertising identifiers through their device settings.

Users may also manage ad personalization settings through Google or their device privacy settings, depending on the operating system and region.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the App’s data handling practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Any changes will be posted on this page.
