# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026.09.14
서울마블 (“the App”) respects your privacy.
The App is a casual board and dice game. The App does not require user accounts, login, registration, or user-provided personal information.
However, the App displays advertisements, and advertising partners may collect or process certain information to provide, measure, and improve ads.

---

## Information We Collect
The App itself does not directly collect personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Address
* Precise location
* User account information

The App may store game-related data locally on your device, such as:

* Game progress
* Scores
* Game settings
* Gameplay preferences
* Unlocked content
* Other game-related progress data

This locally stored data is used only to provide and maintain the game experience and is not used by the App to personally identify users.

* Device information

* Advertising performance information
* Diagnostic information

This information may be used to:

* Display advertisements
* Limit repeated advertisements
* Measure advertising performance
* Detect fraud and abuse
* Improve advertising services
* Provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on user settings, region, consent, and applicable privacy regulations

For more information about how Google handles data, please refer to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google’s advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## App Functionality

The App is a board and dice game inspired by city-based property and travel gameplay.

The App may include features such as:

* Dice-based movement
* Virtual locations and properties
* In-game currency
* Events and random outcomes
* Scores and game progress
* Unlockable content

All locations, game rules, currencies, events, and outcomes are provided for entertainment purposes only.

Any references to real-world places are used as part of the game experience and do not represent real property ownership, investment, financial, or travel services.

## Data Security

The App does not operate its own user account system and does not store user-provided personal information on developer-operated servers.

Users may also manage advertising personalization and privacy settings through Google or their device settings, depending on the operating system and region.
