Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 16, 2026
생생한 생물 대백과 / Living World Encyclopedia ("the App") respects your privacy and is designed as an educational encyclopedia about animals, dinosaurs and prehistoric creatures, and aquatic life.
The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, register, or submit personal information to browse its educational content. The App includes optional rewarded video advertisements that users can choose to watch to unlock additional encyclopedia categories.
This policy describes how the App handles information and how third-party advertising services may process data.
Information We Collect
The App does not intentionally request or directly collect personal details such as:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Home or mailing address
- Precise geographic location
- Account credentials
The App may save limited app-related information locally on your device, including:
- Which encyclopedia categories have been unlocked
- Rewarded-ad unlock status
- App settings and preferences
- Reading or browsing progress, if this feature is enabled
This local information is used to provide and maintain the App's functionality. It is not used by the App to identify you personally and is not sent to our own servers.
Third-party services used by the App may process information as described below.
Advertising and Rewarded Videos
The App uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including optional rewarded video ads.
Some encyclopedia categories are available without viewing an ad. For other categories, users can choose whether to watch a rewarded video advertisement to unlock access. An ad will only be requested after the user chooses the ad-viewing option. A category is unlocked only after the advertising service confirms that the reward has been earned. The unlock status may be saved locally so that the category stays accessible on that device.
Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:
- Advertising identifiers, where permitted
- Device and operating system information
- IP address and approximate location inferred from it
- Ad views, interactions, and reward events
- Ad performance, fraud-prevention, and diagnostic information
Depending on applicable laws, consent, age-related settings, and the advertising configuration, this information may be used to deliver and measure ads, control ad frequency, detect invalid activity, and maintain advertising services. Advertising identifier availability and the types of ads shown may be restricted for children.

Educational Content and App Functionality
The App is a digital encyclopedia featuring:
- Animal Encyclopedia: 120 animals across 8 categories
- Dinosaurs and Prehistoric Life Encyclopedia: 90 creatures across 5 time-period categories
- Underwater Life Encyclopedia: 135 aquatic creatures across 9 categories
The App presents educational descriptions and realistic-style imagery, including digitally created or reconstructed images. Content is intended for general educational and informational purposes; images, especially prehistoric reconstructions, should not be understood as direct documentary photographs of every subject.
The App does not provide medical, veterinary, or other professional advice.
Children's Privacy
The App is intended to be suitable for children and families. The App does not ask children to create accounts or provide contact information, and the developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information directly through the App.
Because the App includes third-party advertising, the advertising provider may process limited device and ad-related data. Where the App is directed to children, appropriate child-directed treatment, age-related restrictions, and family-compliant advertising settings must be applied in accordance with applicable law and Google Play and AdMob requirements. Personalized advertising and advertising identifiers must not be used where prohibited.
Parents and guardians may contact us with questions about children's privacy using the email address below.
Data Storage and Security
The App does not operate its own account database or store user-submitted personal details on developer-operated servers.
App preferences and category unlock status may be stored locally on the device. The availability, retention, and security of data processed by AdMob are governed by Google's applicable policies and safeguards.
No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
User Choices and Data Deletion
Users can choose not to watch rewarded advertisements. Categories that require an ad may remain locked until the user completes the optional reward process.
Depending on their device, region, and age, users or their parents or guardians may be able to manage privacy and advertising settings through Android or Google settings.
Locally stored App data, including saved unlock status, may be deleted by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Doing so may also reset previously unlocked categories.
For requests or questions concerning data processed by Google, please refer to the Google links above. For questions about the App's own data practices, contact us using the address below.
