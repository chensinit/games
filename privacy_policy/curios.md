# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026.10.09**

REWIND (“the App”) respects your privacy.

The App is a casual artifact restoration game featuring restoration requests, artifact cleaning and rust removal, museum exhibition, collection progress, and unlockable restoration content.

The App does not require user accounts, login, registration, or user-provided personal information.

However, the App displays advertisements, and advertising partners may collect or process certain information to provide, measure, and improve ads.

---

## Information We Collect

The App itself does not directly collect personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Address
- Precise location
- User account information

The App may store game-related data locally on your device, such as:

- Game progress
- Restoration progress
- Completed restoration requests
- Restored artifact data
- Museum exhibition progress
- Unlocked artifacts and content
- In-game settings
- Gameplay preferences
- Tutorial progress
- Reward and achievement data

This locally stored data is used only to provide and maintain the game experience and is not used by the App to personally identify users.

## App Functionality

The App is a casual artifact restoration game featuring:

- Receiving restoration requests
- Reading stories related to discovered artifacts
- Removing dust and dirt from artifacts
- Removing rust and corrosion
- Rotating and zooming in on artifacts during restoration
- Completing restoration work
- Displaying restored artifacts in a museum
- Building a collection of restored artifacts
- Unlocking additional artifacts and content

All restoration requests, stories, characters, institutions, locations, events, and related game content are fictionalized or adapted for entertainment purposes unless otherwise stated.

Historical or cultural artifacts represented in the App may be inspired by real-world objects, but the App is not intended to provide academic, archaeological, historical, or professional conservation guidance.

Game progress and related gameplay data may be stored locally on the user's device.

## Data Security

Locally stored game data remains on the user's device unless otherwise stated.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is handled according to their respective privacy and security practices.

## User Choices
