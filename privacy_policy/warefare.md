# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026.07.08

마린키우기 (“the App”) respects your privacy.

The App does not require user accounts, login, registration, or user-provided personal information. However, the App displays advertisements, and advertising partners may collect or process certain information to provide, measure, and improve ads.

---

## Information We Collect

The App itself does not directly collect personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Address
* Precise location
* User account information

---

## Advertising

The App uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information from your device, such as:

* Advertising ID
* Device information
* App interaction information
* Approximate location derived from IP address
* Ad Performance and diagnostic information

### How this information is used:

* Show ads
* Limit repeated ads
* Measure ad performance
* Detect fraud and abuse
* Improve advertising services
* Provide personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on user settings and consent requirements

> For more information about how Google uses data, please refer to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google’s advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Age Restrictions

The App is intended for use by individuals aged 13 or older. The App does not target or knowingly provide services to individuals below this age.

---

## Data Security

The App does not operate its own user account system or store user-provided personal information on its own servers. Information processed by advertising partners is handled according to their respective privacy and security practices.

## User Choices

* Users may also manage ad personalization settings through Google or their device privacy settings, depending on the operating system and region.
